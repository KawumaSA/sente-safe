Act now

If you have already paid or shared your PIN, do these things quickly.

Been scammed? The first hour

Do these things in order. Acting quickly gives you the best chance.

  1. Stop. Do not send more money and do not argue with the scammer. Block the number.
  2. Save the evidence. Screenshot the messages, note the number, the amount, the time and the transaction ID, and keep the confirmation SMS.
  3. Call your mobile money provider. Use the customer care number on its official website or app. Say clearly that you have been defrauded and give the transaction details.
  4. Protect your account. If you shared your PIN or a code, change your PIN straight away. If your phone lost network, tell your provider about a possible SIM swap.
  5. Report to the police. Go to the nearest police station and ask for a reference number for your case.
  6. Escalate if needed. If your provider does not solve it, the Bank of Uganda takes complaints about mobile money [4].
  7. Tell someone. Tell a friend or family member and warn others. Many people are caught, and being scammed does not mean you were foolish.

Getting money back is not guaranteed, but quick reports give your provider and the police the best chance of acting.

Have this ready

  • Your name, phone number and the number that contacted you.
  • The date, time, amount and transaction ID.
  • Screenshots or messages, and a short note of what was said.
