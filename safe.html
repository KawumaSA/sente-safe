Stay safe

A few habits and one checklist stop most scams before they start.

Safe habits

Everyday habits that protect your mobile money.

Your PIN and phone

  • Never share your PIN or the codes sent to you, with anyone.
  • Choose a PIN that is not your birthday or an easy pattern, and change it if you think someone saw it.
  • Lock your phone with a PIN or fingerprint.
  • Do not tap links in unexpected messages. Open the official app yourself.

When you send money

  • Read the name that appears before you confirm. If it is not the person you expect, cancel.
  • Keep the confirmation messages, because you need them if you must report a problem.
  • Pay for a job, scholarship or prize only after you have checked it independently, and in most cases not at all.

At an agent and online

  • Do the transaction on your own phone where you can, and check the amount before you leave.
  • Avoid doing money transactions on public Wi-Fi.
  • Tell a friend or family member about an offer before you act on it.
